Who was Safeerah?

Safeerah started in memory of Safeerah Ullah, a kind, patient and gentle girl who passed away from an unknown and rare cancer two months before her 19th birthday. She taught us what it means to uplift others, show compassion and always have a reason to smile.

She wanted to be a therapist and help others through their troubles with genuine care. As a child she wanted to be a teacher. Growing up, she was passionate about children’s education, supporting orphans and improving the well-being of impoverished communities as a global citizen.

When she was ill, her wish granted from Make-A-Wish was to support orphans through IRUSA. We carry on her legacy with Safeerah

Our purpose

We bring hope, dignity and opportunity to less fortunate children and their families around the world, through education, healthcare and sustainable development programs that transform lives.

Every child deserves a safe, supportive place to live and grow, guided by generosity and humanity. We work where the need is greatest, and alongside others to make lasting change at the local level.

“…whoever saves a life, it is as if he had saved all of mankind.”
