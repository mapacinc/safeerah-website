Our story

A life that taught us how to uplift others

Safeerah Ullah was kind, patient and gentle. She passed away from a rare cancer two months before her 19th birthday, and she taught everyone around her about compassion and always having a reason to smile.

When she was ill, her Make-A-Wish was to support orphans through IRUSA. Safeerah carries that act of selflessness forward in her name.
